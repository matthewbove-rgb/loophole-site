Help, common questions and contact

How to play

Hold a finger anywhere on the screen. The wire draws just above your thumb, so your thumb never covers the action. Circle enemies to close a loop and capture them. Lifting your finger slows time, and after 2 seconds the game pauses.

Restoring LOOPHOLE+

If you bought LOOPHOLE+ and reinstalled the game or got a new iPhone, open Settings → Restore Purchases in the game and sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. LOOPHOLE+ works with Family Sharing.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your iPhone and is included in your iCloud or computer backup. Restoring the iPhone from a backup brings it back. Deleting the app also deletes its progress, unless you restore from a backup made before you deleted it.

No sound

LOOPHOLE follows your iPhone's ring/silent switch. Turn the switch off silent and check the SOUND level in the game's Settings.

Contact

Found a bug or have feedback? Email Loophole.Support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.
