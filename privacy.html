LOOPHOLE

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version: LOOPHOLE does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy covers the LOOPHOLE game for iPhone and its web version. LOOPHOLE is made by Matthew Bove, an independent developer ("we", "us").

Your game data stays on your device

Your progress, records and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. If your device backups are turned on (iCloud or computer backup), this data is included in those backups, which Apple controls under its own privacy policy. You can erase your progress at any time with RESET PROGRESS in the game's Settings, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

The LOOPHOLE+ unlock is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether you own LOOPHOLE+ so it can unlock content and restore your purchase. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center under your Game Center profile so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple runs Game Center, and we do not receive any other information about you. You can sign out of Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Sharing

The SHARE button opens your device's share sheet with a short text about your run. You choose whether and where to send it, and we do not see what you share.

Crash reports and App Store statistics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and aggregate statistics, such as the number of installs. These do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

The web version

The web version is a static page served by GitHub Pages. The game itself sets no cookies and keeps your progress only in your browser's local storage on your device. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

LOOPHOLE is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. All of your game data is on your device and under your control. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If we ever add a feature that collects data, such as analytics, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: Loophole.Support@gmail.com
